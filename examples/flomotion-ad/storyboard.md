# Storyboard: flomotion ad

Hero: flo, a dot with eyes (on its own track) · Turn: the two-dots turn at 7.0 s (marker `turn-marker` at 8.9 s) · Tempo: 128 bpm

## 1. Hook · 0-3.28 s

- Block: Hook
- Scene: hook
- Voice: "Stop editing videos. Start describing them!"
- On screen: Stop editing. (struck through) Start describing. A prompt types in: a 30 s ad for flomotion
- Motion: the strike draws on after "videos"; the highlight wipes behind "describing"; the prompt card rises
- Sound: whoosh + hit on "Start", type ticks
- Why it works: contrast hook with no brand name; the typed prompt is paid off in the CTA

## 2. Pain · 3.28-7.03 s

- Block: Problem
- Scene: pain-1, pain-2, pain-3, pain-4
- Voice: "Timelines." "Dragging." "Exporting." "Again."
- On screen: one word per beat on a blue, green, purple, red block
- Motion: slab, vertical, iris and side wipes between the words; "Again." glitches and shakes
- Sound: whip on every cut, a glitch whip on the last
- Why it works: four specific verbs of the old way, rule of four

## 3. Turn · 7.03-8.91 s

- Block: Bridge
- Scene: turn
- Voice: (silent)
- On screen: two big dots converge into a small ringed dot, split into two small dots, grow into the next slide
- Motion: converge 0.8 s, merge, split 0.3 s, grow with power3.in
- Sound: the music ducks, a hit on the merge, a pop on the split, riser and hit land on the drop
- Why it works: a match cut on a circle; the story turns without a word

## 4. Promise · 8.91-11.72 s

- Block: ProductReveal
- Scene: promise
- Voice: "Meet flow-motion. One T S X file."
- On screen: Meet flomotion; a vault door opens onto a yellow disc; One TSX file.
- Motion: the door lands, its wheel spins, it shrinks and turns away
- Sound: hit when it lands, tick for the unlock, whip when it opens
- Why it works: the circle from the turn becomes the door; the line is a fact

## 5. Product · 11.72-15.0 s

- Block: UIZoom
- Scene: product
- Voice: "Claude builds it." "You watch it come alive."
- On screen: the real live view in a browser frame; the activity feed floats out; an "alive" chip
- Motion: the frame rises with a bounce, the card slides out and scrolls
- Sound: hit, pop, chime
- Why it works: shows the real thing, animated

## 7. Checks · 18.28-21.09 s

## 8. Licences · 21.09-24.375 s

## 10. CTA · 27.19-30.0 s
