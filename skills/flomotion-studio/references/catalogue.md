# Reference catalogue (pattern source)

Source: ~60 still frames the user collected from one compilation video of "best SaaS promo videos". Frames only: **no timing, no sound, no performance data**. Rankings in the overlay are the compilation's, not a quality measure (the compilation has low view counts).

Use this file to learn **patterns** (structure, pacing logic, hierarchy). Never reproduce a brand's assets, footage, copy or exact layouts. Brand names appear here only as citations of where a pattern was observed.

Confidence: `H` clearly visible, `M` inferred from stills, `L` guess.

## Ad A: Ansarada (data rooms) — Metaphor contrast
Beats: (1) grainy B/W roulette wheel, red tint [pain as image] -> (2) vertical split-wipe match-cut: wheel centre to vault-door centre, dark to white [turn] -> (3) black text on white "Hello simple and secure data rooms" [promise] -> (4) white 3D vault, door opening onto a glowing lime disc [reveal] -> (5) tilted laptop with skeleton UI (grey bars + colored pills) [product] -> (6) same UI on phone [multi-device] -> (7) black logo on white [brand] -> (8) "First data room to be ISO 27001:2022 certified" [credential proof].
Design: dark gritty vs white clean; single lime accent; big left-aligned neo-grotesque; 3D render for the vault. Confidence H on structure, M on timing.
Lessons: pain can be an image, not words; a match-cut on shared geometry makes the turn feel designed; skeleton UI hides complexity; proof is a concrete credential.

## Ad B: Slack — Typographic color blocks
Beats: logo + parent lockup -> typed search query in a purple search field ("Is Slack changing the way businesses do business?") [curiosity hook] -> customer-name swap "Just ask [logo] Roku" over blurred footage -> customer quote cards on solid colors (pink, green) with key words in white, attribution lockup (logo, name, title) -> "And [logo] Canva", "And [logo] Signable" roll call over b-roll -> magnifier sweeping a word to reveal white letters on blue -> colorful UI-card collage tight crop -> logo assembled from large colored shapes.
Design: one flat color per scene, giant geometric sans, cropped letters at frame edges, palette taken from the logo colors; UI gestures (cursor, selection box, magnifier) applied to type.
Lessons: proof from other people's voices; one color world per scene; key word in white, lead-in in dark; logo-in-sentence swap; brand assembled from motion shapes.

## Ad C: ClickUp — Pain-to-calm line story
Beats: white screen "Work Sprawl is killing us." over unreadable diagram of boxes and arrows -> thick black line with round head sweeping with speed streaks -> "Too many apps…" icons tangled on the line with red badges -> dark wedge wipe to white -> dark starry "Too many AI tools…" line looping through logos -> "Context is lost." with question bubbles along the line -> thesis "Work sprawl kills human productivity" (second line swapped to "AI productivity"), line's dot belongs to a letter -> "It's time to kill sprawl." on pale gradient, line ends as the period -> "kill Sprawl" struck with scanline glitch -> saturated magenta/orange/purple gradient with grain, product cards flying with heavy motion blur -> grid of mini-app cards -> "A New Era of AI" (soft star blob) -> "A New Era of Craft & Quality" (cards orbiting text) -> logo + version badge, "AI Workspace" with motion blur -> tilted isometric card wall.
Design: pain half is monochrome and thin; solution half is saturated, grained, 3D tilt and blur.
Lessons: a **single continuous motif** (the line) ties scenes; pain takes about half the runtime in several distinct flavors; palette flip marks the turn; template reuse with word swap; text strike for "kill".

## Ad D: Vaylens (EV charging software) — Trend-to-product explainer
Beats: flat blue "As the need to use electric cars becomes the norm" (current word bright, others dim) -> pale screen, photo framed in the middle, text flanking left and right ("the way we [photo] recharge them") -> two blue circles close in, "we built" -> white blob pinches and morphs into the wordmark -> gradient "It's a charging management software" -> frosted-glass dashboard over blurred top-down footage of a team -> mosaic of industry footage with "helping thousands of companies".
Design: one electric blue + pale lavender; light-weight type, generous space; circles/blob as transitions; footage in clean framed rectangles.
Lessons: open from an agreeable market fact; split one sentence across scenes; shapes as punctuation; category line in plain words; glass UI placed over real-world context; breadth proof as a mosaic.

## Ad E: Monday.com — Real-UI demo
Beats: person talking to camera in office -> logo on a frosted glass pill -> real board UI with colored status/priority cells -> dashboard widgets (progress bar, revenue vs goal, trend line) -> AI sidekick chat ("Please collect all live Q4 projects into one board" / "Sure, I'm on it!") -> board builds itself: empty cells become filled colored cells via circle-to-cell morphs, avatars slide in.
Design: close crops of real UI; saturated status color blocks; pastel gradient; glass.
Lessons: demo-led; plain-language AI request/response; shape morph as progress; color-coded cells make the product recognizable.

## Ad F: Canva (video) — Human story to prompt
Beats: woman chopping vegetables with a phone in her mouth filming herself, script logotype overlaid [funny, relatable pain] -> wide shot with ring light -> overhead hands with purple selection frame, green cursor, name tag "Masha" [multiplayer cue] -> white prompt box "What's the topic of your video?" typing, four thumbnails of the footage just seen, option chips ("Playful", "Montage") -> teal-to-violet gradient, "[logo] Video" in a white pill.
Lessons: story first, product as a simple prompt; footage becomes the UI input; cursor name tag as character.

## Ad G: Phantom (wallet) — 3D object explainer (frames labelled "#8 Google Gemini" in the overlay; the label and brand disagree, treat as Phantom)
Beats: three plain grey desktop windows (connect, collections, gas fee) [the old, complicated way] -> violet screen "a home for your NFTs" over a black circle with a glassy 3D NFT frame and props -> phone UI long-press menu with "Pin it!" bubble and touch ripple -> black "one wallet" with 3D bits orbiting -> 3D purple wallet with app cards -> "drag" with floating coins and large blobs -> "a wallet you'll love" with radial black dashes -> black "phantom.app".
Design: electric violet alternating with pure black; big lowercase geometric sans in white; glossy 3D props; burst graphic for impact.
Lessons: old way small and grey, new way big and colored; 2 to 4 word lines; gesture callouts; URL end card.
